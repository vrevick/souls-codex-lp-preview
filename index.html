Questions before you begin

Read this with your eyes open.

How personal is Soul’s Codex?

The book combines shared explanations of each symbolic system with 38 interpretations generated around your name, birth details and reflective inputs. It is personalized, but not every educational page is uniquely written for one reader.

Is this fortune-telling?

No. Soul’s Codex is designed as a symbolic framework for reflection. It explores themes and possible expressions; it does not predict fixed events or remove your freedom to choose.

What if I am skeptical?

Bring the doubt with you. The book itself encourages you to read, observe and test ideas against lived experience. You do not need to accept every interpretation for the process to be useful.

What information is used?

Your name, date, time and place of birth form the central inputs. The current book also draws on personal answers used to shape practical context.

Is this professional advice?

No. The interpretations are symbolic and educational. They are not a substitute for medical, psychological, legal, financial or other professional advice.
